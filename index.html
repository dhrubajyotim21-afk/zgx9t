ABOUT & BIO

Beyond the Legend • কিংবদন্তিৰ আঁৰৰ মানুহজন

জুবিন গাৰ্গৰ জন্মনাম আছিল জুবিন বৰঠাকুৰ। পৰৱৰ্তী সময়ত তেওঁ নিজৰ Brahmin gotra “Garg”-ৰ পৰা “Garg” নামটো গ্ৰহণ কৰে। তেওঁৰ পৰিচয় আৰু সৃষ্টিশীলতাত অসমীয়া সংস্কৃতি আৰু পৰম্পৰাৰ শিপাৰ সৈতে বিশ্ব সংগীতৰ প্ৰভাৱৰ এক সুন্দৰ সমন্বয় দেখা যায়।

Milestones of a Musical Journey • সংগীতৰ বাটত গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ মাইলষ্টোন

1992 — The Beginning সংগীতৰ যাত্ৰাৰ আৰম্ভণি নৱেম্বৰ ১৯৯২ত প্ৰথম অসমীয়া album ‘Anamika’-ৰ জৰিয়তে তেওঁৰ সংগীত যাত্ৰাই এক নতুন অধ্যায়ত প্ৰৱেশ কৰে।
1995 — Bollywood Entry অসমৰ পৰা বহল সংগীতৰ পৃথিৱীলৈ মুম্বাইলৈ যাত্ৰা কৰি ‘Chandni Raat’ Indipop album-ৰ জৰিয়তে Bollywood music scene-ত আত্মপ্ৰকাশ কৰে।
2003 — Into Bengali Cinema বাংলা সংগীত জগতত প্ৰৱেশ ‘Mon’ ছবিৰ জৰিয়তে বাংলা চলচ্চিত্ৰৰ সংগীত জগতত তেওঁৰ যাত্ৰা আৰম্ভ হয়।
2006 — The Song That Changed Everything এটা গানে সলনি কৰা এটা অধ্যায় ‘Gangster’ ছবিৰ ‘Ya Ali’ গানে তেওঁক Bollywood-ত ব্যাপক জনপ্ৰিয়তা প্ৰদান কৰে আৰু তেওঁৰ সংগীত যাত্ৰাৰ এক উল্লেখযোগ্য milestone হৈ পৰے۔

FAREWELL

A Hero’s Final Journey

When a legend leaves this world, the music never truly fades. It continues to live through memories, melodies, and the hearts of those who loved him.

Zubeen Da’s final farewell became a powerful reflection of the extraordinary love and respect he received from millions.

His farewell achieved the Guinness World Record for the 4th Largest Funeral Gathering in history, reflecting the immense place he held in the hearts of people across the world.

As Assam bid farewell to its beloved son, “Mayabini” echoed through the air—turning the final goodbye into an unforgettable moment.

STATE HONOURS

21-Gun Salute He was laid to rest with full state honours, including a ceremonial 21-gun salute.
Three Days of Mourning Assam CM Himanta Biswa Sarma declared three days of state mourning.
CM in Attendance Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma attended the final rites at Kamarkuchi NC village.
A Massive Public Farewell More than 15 lakh people gathered at Sarusajai Stadium to pay their final respects.

FINAL RITES

The final rites were performed by his younger sister, Palmi Borthakur, alongside close associates.

Following a second post-mortem, his body was taken to Sarusajai Stadium and later to the cremation ground.

Thousands of admirers gathered at Kamarkuchi NC village, near Guwahati, to say their final goodbye.

As the beloved song “Mayabini” played during the farewell, fans joined together in chorus.

His Final Resting Place

শেষ বিশ্ৰামৰ স্থান

Zubeen Garg was laid to rest at Sonapur.

The place is now known as “Zubeen Khetra”, a place that stands as a lasting symbol of remembrance and respect for the legendary artist.
